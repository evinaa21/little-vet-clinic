# Drop the animal art here

Put the nine PNGs in this folder, then run:

```bash
./scripts/import_faces.sh
```

That trims each one to its artwork, scales them all to a common size, centres
them on a square 256×256 canvas, and installs them into the asset catalog under
`AnimalFaces`. Rebuild (⌘R) and they're in the app.

## The nine files

| File | Where it shows up |
|---|---|
| `dog_waiting.png` `cat_waiting.png` `bunny_waiting.png` | A patient still on the board |
| `dog_seen.png` `cat_seen.png` `bunny_seen.png` | The same patient, checked off |
| `dog_celebrating.png` `cat_celebrating.png` `bunny_celebrating.png` | The `CLINIC CLOSED FOR TODAY` sheet only |

Names must match exactly — lowercase, underscore, `.png`.

## What each one should be

- **Any canvas size — the import normalises them.** Illustrations never come back
  on a shared canvas, so `import_faces.sh` trims each to its alpha bounds, scales
  it so its longest side is the same fraction of the sheet as every other face,
  and centres it on 256×256. Give it at least ~500px on the long side and the
  downscale stays crisp on Retina.
- **Transparent background**, or plain white with the background removed before
  you drop it in here. The trim reads the alpha channel, so a face on an opaque
  white rectangle can't be trimmed and will import at the wrong scale.
- **Face only.** No body, no props.
- **No circle behind the face.** The pastel disc is drawn by the app as a plain
  SwiftUI `Circle`, and its colour cycles per row independently of the animal. A
  circle baked into the PNG will sit inside that one and look like a target.
- **Keep everything inside a centred circle.** The badge is round and the face is
  inset about 7% inside it, so anything out near the corners of the square canvas
  gets cropped. Sparkles, ear tips and confetti need to sit well in from the edge.

The three moods want to read at a glance at 34 pixels: `waiting` neutral or
sleepy, `seen` content and pleased, `celebrating` openly delighted. Keep the line
weight and the head size consistent across all nine or the rows will look like
they came from different sets.

Until real files land here, the app uses flat placeholder faces generated by
`scripts/make_art.swift`.
